About the Project

Turning supply chain data into actionable business intelligence.

Data-Driven Supply Chain Management

The project analyzes supply chain and logistics data to identify delivery delays, inventory risks, transportation patterns, warehouse performance and operational risks.

Machine learning and business intelligence techniques are combined to provide predictive insights and support better operational decision-making.

  • Delivery performance analysis
  • Inventory monitoring
  • Warehouse analysis
  • Transportation cost analysis
  • Delivery risk monitoring
  • Predictive delay analysis

Supply Chain Control Tower

The Power BI dashboard provides a centralized analytical view of supply chain operations, allowing users to monitor KPIs, delivery status, inventory levels, logistics costs and risk levels.


The predictive analytics page extends the dashboard with machine-learning-based delivery delay analysis.
